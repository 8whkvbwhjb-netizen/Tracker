Lantern Keep

Light the four lanterns in each room of the keep, take the stair down, and get out before the Keeper wakes. Built to the rules of a 1980 home console: 160 × 96 pixels, 16 colours, eight moving objects and a keypad overlay.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD move · Space fires · 1 flare · 3 bell · P pause · M sound · Enter start

Lantern Keep

Disc

From the instruction booklet

Object

Each room of the keep holds four cold lanterns. Walk into a lantern to light it. When all four burn, a stair appears deep in the room. Step onto it to go down to the next room.

The bar in the top right corner is the Keeper's sleep. When it runs out, the Keeper steps out of the wall and hunts you. Arrows only stun it, so light the lanterns quickly.

The heartbeat you hear speeds up as the bar drains.

Built inside the console's limits

  • 160 × 96 pixels on a 20 × 12 grid of 8 × 8 background cards. Walls, lanterns, stairs and pickups are all cards.
  • The fixed 16-colour palette and nothing else.
  • Eight moving objects at most: you, your arrow, the Keeper and five creatures. That cap is why only one arrow flies at a time.
  • Every moving object is a single colour. The wraith flickers on alternate frames, the old trick for ghosts.
  • Three square-wave voices plus a noise channel, like the console's sound chip.
